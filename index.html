ABOUT THE ARTIST

Obsessed with detail,
devoted to texture.

I'm Hamid — a traditional and contemporary artist based in Pakistan, specializing in detailed botanical sketches, Arabic calligraphy with gold accents, and 3D textured wall art. My work lives at the intersection of old-world craftsmanship and modern minimalism.

Each piece begins with graphite — often 8B on textured canvas paper — slowly building layers until the leaf breathes, the script glows, or the texture catches light like plaster at golden hour. I don't chase trends; I create heirlooms for quiet, light-filled spaces.
